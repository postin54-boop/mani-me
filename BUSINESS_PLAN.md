# Mani Me Business Plan
**UK to Ghana Parcel Delivery & E-Commerce Platform**

---

## Executive Summary

**Company Name:** Mani Me  
**Website:** www.manime.co.uk  
**Industry:** Logistics & E-Commerce  
**Business Model:** B2C Parcel Delivery & Online Retail  
**Target Market:** UK-based Ghanaian diaspora and Ghana residents  

Mani Me is a technology-driven logistics company specializing in parcel delivery services between the United Kingdom and Ghana. Our platform combines door-to-door parcel collection in the UK with last-mile delivery across Ghana, complemented by an integrated e-commerce grocery shop.

### Mission Statement
To make sending parcels from the UK to Ghana as easy, reliable, and affordable as possible — connecting families across continents.

### Vision Statement
To become the leading UK-Ghana logistics provider, trusted by the diaspora community for seamless parcel delivery and e-commerce services.

### Value Proposition
- **Convenience:** Door-to-door pickup anywhere in the UK
- **Reliability:** Real-time tracking and 4-5 week delivery guarantee
- **Transparency:** No hidden fees, clear pricing structure
- **Technology:** Modern mobile apps for customers and drivers
- **Trust:** 100% insurance coverage on all shipments

## Operations Plan

### Service Workflow

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                           CUSTOMER JOURNEY                                │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                    │
                                    ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. BOOKING                                                               │
│    Customer books via mobile app → Selects parcel size → Enters details │
│    → Chooses pickup date/time → Makes payment (card/cash/Apple Pay)     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                    │
                                    ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 2. UK PICKUP                                                             │
│    UK Driver assigned → Navigates to customer → Collects parcel         │
│    → Prints label → Delivers to UK warehouse                            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                    │
                                    ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 3. WAREHOUSE PROCESSING (UK)                                             │
│    Parcel received → Sorted → Packed → Customs documentation            │
│    → Shipped to Ghana (sea/air freight)                                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                    │
                                    ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 4. TRANSIT & CUSTOMS                                                     │
│    In transit to Ghana → Arrives at port → Customs clearance            │
│    → Delivered to Ghana warehouse                                       │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                    │
                                    ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 5. GHANA DELIVERY                                                        │
│    Ghana driver assigned → Picks up from warehouse → Delivers to        │
│    recipient → Captures proof of delivery (photo) → Marked as delivered │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Shipment Status Tracking
1. **Booked** - Customer completed booking
2. **Pending Pickup** - Awaiting driver assignment
3. **Driver Assigned** - UK driver allocated
4. **Driver En Route** - Driver heading to pickup
5. **Picked Up** - Parcel collected
6. **At UK Warehouse** - Arrived at UK hub
7. **Processing** - Being sorted/packed
8. **Departed UK** - Shipped from UK
9. **In Transit** - On the way to Ghana
10. **Arrived Ghana** - At Ghana warehouse
11. **Customs** - Clearance process
12. **Customs Cleared** - Released from customs
13. **Out for Delivery** - Ghana driver delivering
14. **Delivered** - Successfully delivered

### Warehouse Operations

#### UK Warehouse
- Parcel receiving from drivers
- Inventory management
- Sorting by destination region
- Packing for international shipping
- Customs documentation preparation
- Freight coordination

#### Ghana Warehouse
- Receiving international shipments

- Customer pickup option

### Technology Infrastructure

#### Mobile Applications
- **Customer App (mani-me-mobile):** iOS & Android via Expo

- **Authentication:** JWT + Firebase Auth
- **Notifications:** Expo Push + Firebase Cloud Messaging
- **Payments:** Stripe integration (card, Apple Pay)

### Quality Assurance

- Photo documentation at pickup
